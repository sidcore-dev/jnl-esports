About the organization

A competitive organization that begins with Rainbow Six Siege.

JNL Esports is a competitive organization that begins with Rainbow Six Siege. Our focus is on building one roster properly before expanding into additional titles.

As the organization grows, each new game will have its own dedicated roster and staff, held to the same standards.

Structured practice
Scheduled scrims, VOD review and clearly defined roles.
Professional conduct
Respectful communication and accountability from every member.
Player development
A defined path to the main roster for players who perform.
